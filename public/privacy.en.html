Privacy & data

Last updated: 18 Nov 2026 · Aely (Juanjo Avila)

What we store

  • Your portfolio: accounts, investments, debts, budget and settings (on your device and, if you sign in, in Supabase).
  • Expenses: amount, merchant, category and date (expenses table).
  • Bank connections: Open Banking link status and encrypted session tokens. We never store your bank password.
  • MyInvestor: encrypted session tokens after connecting. The password is only used to sign in and is not stored.
  • Diagnostics and usage: while you are signed in, the app sends errors (cleaned before they leave), which screens are used (once per session) and how long syncs take, together with the app version and platform. Usage metrics never include amounts, merchants or anything you type. This cannot be turned off today.

Where it lives

Cloud data is stored in Supabase (EU). The web app is served from GitHub Pages. Each user can only read and write their own data (Row Level Security), with two exceptions: if you share a Household, its members see the balances you share, and the app administrator reads the diagnostics and usage data.

Optional advanced help

“Ask me” works locally and offline. If you choose to turn on advanced help, only the question you typed is sent to OpenAI. No balances, accounts, transactions or portfolio contents are attached. You are asked for consent before the first message and can withdraw it in Settings. Do not type account or card numbers, passwords, PINs, CVVs, keys or tokens; the app blocks them when it spots those patterns.

Your rights

  • Access & portability: all your data can be seen inside the app, and Settings → Backup keeps a daily copy you can restore. If you want a full copy as a file, ask for it at the contact email.
  • Erasure: Settings → Your account → Delete my account (asks for your password). Removes your user and all its data.
  • Rectification: you can edit any data inside the app.
